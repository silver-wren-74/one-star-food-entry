Entry for the [One Star, Five Stars for the Food](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7)
competition on make.fun: one sentence (at most 200 characters) in the
voice of an alien tourist giving planet Earth a one-star review while
still admitting the food was excellent.

`main.py` writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`:

> One star: your gravity confiscates luggage, your natives queue at
> dawn for bitter burnt-bean water and call the trembling
> "productivity" — but your tacos al pastor nearly made me overstay
> my visa.
